# PlanSight: 2-minute video pitch

The hard limit is 2:00; the build stops at 1:55. The video is mostly **the live app**, with three slides to frame it: the jury sees the product working before they see a single architecture box.

`PlanSight_pitch_draft.mp4` (1:40) is generated by `build/build.py`. It cuts five screen recordings of the app (`build/footage/*.mp4`, made by `build/record_app.py`) between slides, with a synthetic voice. Upload it as is, or record your own voice over the same cut (see the end of this file).

- **CubiCasa doors** is the one case where Nemotron's boxes are invented, so it is the problem shot. Do not use it as a success.
- **The Finnish sinks question** on 4017 is answered correctly: 2, ✓.
- **Swiss doors** comes from the DXF: 36, ✓, 0 tokens.
- **The KPI cards** describe the 805-question sample (55% final accuracy, 85% when it answers). The narration quotes only the per-dataset figures, which hold on the full data.

## Rebuilding the video

```bash
.venv/bin/pip install -e '.[app]' playwright             # app + recorder (uses your installed Chrome)
.venv/bin/python scripts/warm_demo.py --model nemotron_nvidia   # cache every sample, so takes are instant
.venv/bin/python project_submission/build/record_app.py        # 5 clips -> build/footage, stills -> assets/app_*.png
.venv/bin/python project_submission/build/build.py             # deck PDF + PlanSight_pitch_draft.mp4
```

- `record_app.py` starts its own `drawingqa serve` on a free port. It drives Chrome with Playwright, draws a visible cursor, and captures 1920×1080 at 30 fps. Use `--only live_ask` to redo a single scene, or `--headed` to watch it.
- `build.py` holds each clip's last frame until the narration ends. If a clip is missing, it falls back to the matching slide. Use `--slides-only` to ignore the footage entirely.
- The Benchmark tab needs `runs/per-folder-6-v2` locally. The live scenes need the cache warmed by `warm_demo.py`; otherwise they call the NVIDIA API on camera, which takes 5–10 s per detection.

## Recording your own voice

1. Read the narration column at a calm pace (about 160 words per minute). Record on a phone in a quiet room, about 20 cm away.
2. In iMovie or CapCut, lay your voice over the draft video and mute the synthetic track. Alternatively, rebuild the cut from `build/footage/*.mp4` and the slide PNGs, following the order above.
3. If you would rather show the app live, run `.venv/bin/drawingqa serve`, open <http://127.0.0.1:8000/> in Chrome at 125% zoom, and follow the same clicks. Check that the dot next to the model picker is green. <http://127.0.0.1:8000/#bench> opens the Benchmark tab directly.
4. Export, and check the length is **≤ 1:55**. Upload to YouTube as *Public* (or *Unlisted*, if the form accepts it), and test the link in a private window.
   Title: `PlanSight – Make Blueprints Talk | Junction 2026 Stoa challenge | NVIDIA Nemotron`.
