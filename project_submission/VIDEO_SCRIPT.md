# PlanProof: 2-minute video pitch

The judges will be strict: **the hard limit is 2:00, so aim for 1:50**. The script below has about 250 words, which takes about 1:45 at a calm pace. A draft video that follows this script is generated by `build/build_video.sh`, using the slides and a synthetic voice: `PlanProof_pitch_draft.mp4`. Use it as a timing reference, or upload it if there is no time to record. A real voice and a screen recording of the report will score better.

## Shot list and narration

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Title slide: PlanProof, Stoa, NVIDIA Nemotron | "Site engineers lose hours every day hunting through drawings. Stoa answers project questions with the page behind every sentence. We built PlanProof to give the symbol behind every number." |
| 0:12–0:27 | The flagged CubiCasa example: six boxes in a straight column | "Ask a vision model how many doors are on this plan, and it may say six, and even be right, but look at its boxes: a straight column of doors that don't exist. You can't trust a number you can't check." |
| 0:27–0:50 | Architecture slide: three routes | "PlanProof routes every question to the most reliable source. Many drawings ship with their CAD or BIM file. When we find the DXF or the IFC model, we answer from it directly: zero tokens, deterministic, every element mapped back onto the sheet. For scans, NVIDIA Nemotron Nano Omni returns an inventory of boxes, and Python does the counting." |
| 0:50–1:10 | Screen recording of `demo/index.html`: filter by dataset, click an answer, show the numbered boxes and the route; then an IFC answer with GlobalIds | "Every answer opens in a review report: the page, numbered boxes, a one-line reason, and which route answered. Suspicious patterns are flagged as needs review, not guessed. A human verifies in seconds." |
| 1:10–1:27 | Results and cost slides | "On held-out data: 99 percent on Swiss plans, 100 percent on more than three thousand IFC questions in nine languages. A third of all questions cost zero tokens. The rest run on Nemotron's three billion active parameters, about a thousand tokens per question, and its open weights can be self-hosted in the EU." |
| 1:27–1:45 | Detector slide: Swiss held-out vs CubiCasa | "And this is what won't be obsolete next year: we turned CAD files into free training labels. Our tiny detector already finds the real doors on Finnish scans it has never seen. Every CAD file Stoa connects makes it better." |
| 1:45–1:52 | Closing slide with links | "PlanProof. Every number, with the proof drawn on it." |

## Recording tips

- Record the screen at 1920×1080. Open `project_submission/demo/index.html` in Chrome and zoom the browser to 125%.
- In the report, click the dataset filter, pick `swiss_dwellings`, open the "How many doors" answer, and zoom into the boxes. Then pick `ifc_models` and open a room-area answer.
- Record the voice separately (phone in a quiet room, ~20 cm away), then lay it over the slides and the screen recording in iMovie or CapCut.
- Check the final length in the export dialog: **≤ 1:55**. Upload to YouTube as *Public* (or *Unlisted*, if the form accepts it), with no age restriction. Test the link in a private browser window.
- Title: `PlanProof – Make Blueprints Talk | Junction 2026 Stoa challenge | NVIDIA Nemotron`.
